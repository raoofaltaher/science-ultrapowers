# Access requests for the References & Resources Specialist (your human partner)

The procedure is in `science-ultrapowers:requesting-reference-access`. Type is `FULLTEXT`, `CONFIRM_DOI` or `APPROVAL`; priority is `P1` (blocks a gate), `P2` (this cycle) or `P3` (background); status runs `OPEN` then `FULFILLED`, `APPROVED`, `DECLINED` or `UNAVAILABLE`. Delivered PDFs are saved to `references/<FirstAuthor>_<Year>_<ShortTitle>.pdf`.
