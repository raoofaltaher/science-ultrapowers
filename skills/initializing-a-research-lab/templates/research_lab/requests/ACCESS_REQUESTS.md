# Access requests for the Principal Researcher (the researcher)

The procedure is in `science-ultrapowers:requesting-reference-access`. Type is `full-text request`, `DOI confirmation` or `exception approval`; priority is `blocks a stage`, `this cycle` or `background`; status runs `open`, then `fulfilled`, `approved`, `declined` or `unavailable`. Delivered PDFs are saved to `references/<FirstAuthor>_<Year>_<ShortTitle>.pdf`.
