# Decisions

`DECISION_LOG.md` records every stage approval and scope decision (id, date, stage, decision, rationale, evidence, approver), and `lab_meetings/` holds the session openings, session closes, lab meeting notes and stage approval notes written from the templates in `../templates/`. A decision is cited by its id and never edited once logged; a later decision supersedes it. The Academic Supervisor records the decisions they approve and the Research Project Manager records the stage approval and meeting notes; the meeting routine is in `science-ultrapowers:holding-lab-meetings`.
