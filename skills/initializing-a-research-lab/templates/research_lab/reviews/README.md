# Reviews

`REVIEW_LOG.md` logs every review and stage approval, and `reports/` holds the review reports, referee reports, adversarial reviews and the citation audit write-ups they point at. Nobody reviews their own work, and a not-cleared audit, a blocking grade or a "reject" blocks its stage approval until it is resolved and re-reviewed. The Academic Reviewer, the Scientific Peer Reviewer and the Citation & Reference Auditor write the reports and log them; the Research Project Manager checks that every stage approval has its log row.
