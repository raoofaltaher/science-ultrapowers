# Decisions

`DECISIONS.md` logs every gate decision and scope decision (id, context, options, decision, rationale, evidence ids, approver), and `lab_meetings/` holds the session kickoffs, stand-ups, lab meeting notes and gate-review notes written from the templates in `../templates/`. A decision is cited by its id and never edited once logged; a later decision supersedes it. The Academic Supervisor records the decisions they approve and the Research Project Manager records the gate and meeting notes; the meeting routine is in `science-ultrapowers:holding-lab-meetings`.
