# Reference registry

`REGISTRY.csv` lists every source the lab has admitted or evaluated, with its tier, DOI verification status and evidence cards; `EXCEPTIONS.md` logs the Tier B approvals and attribution-only preprint citations; `evidence_cards/` holds one card per claim taken from a source; `audits/` holds the citation audit reports. A source is citable only when its row is `ADMITTED` (or `ADMITTED_EXCEPTION`) and an evidence card supports the exact sentence; the rules and statuses are in `science-ultrapowers:verifying-evidence-by-doi`. The Literature Researcher writes the registry rows and evidence cards, the Findings & Records Curator re-verifies them, and the Citation & Reference Auditor audits the folder at every gate; Tier B approvals come from your human partner as References & Resources Specialist.
