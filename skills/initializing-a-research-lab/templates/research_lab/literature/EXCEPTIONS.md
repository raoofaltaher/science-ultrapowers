# Approved exceptions and attribution-only preprints

A peer-reviewed work without a DOI is admitted only after the official proceedings page is checked and the researcher approves it here, before the source is cited; request approval through `requests/` (type exception approval).

| REQ-id (request) | Source (first author, year, title, venue) | Official proceedings URL | Why no DOI | Why no peer-reviewed source with a matched DOI | Approved by | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Attribution-only preprints

A preprint is never evidence. It may be cited only to credit a method's or idea's origin, written `[Author Year, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>)` next to what it credits, and listed here.

| Preprint (first author, year, identifier) | Method or idea credited | Where cited (file and location) | Listed by and date |
