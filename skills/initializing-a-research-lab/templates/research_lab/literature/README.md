# Literature

`SOURCES.csv` is the source table: it lists every source the lab has admitted or evaluated, with its source class, DOI status and evidence records. `EXCEPTIONS.md` records the approved exceptions and the attribution-only preprint citations. `evidence_records/` holds one record per claim taken from a source, `search_logs/` holds the logs of each literature search, and `citation_audits/` holds the citation audit reports. A source is citable only when its row has the status citable (or citable by exception) and an evidence record supports the exact sentence; the rules and statuses are in `science-ultrapowers:verifying-evidence-by-doi`. The Literature Researcher writes the source rows and evidence records, the Findings & Records Curator re-checks them, and the Citation & Reference Auditor audits the folder at every stage approval; approvals of works without a DOI come from the researcher as Principal Researcher.

The `status` column of `SOURCES.csv` holds one of: citable, citable by exception, metadata matched, awaiting full text, awaiting approval, candidate, excluded: preprint, excluded: retracted, rejected: mismatch. The `source_class` column holds one of: peer-reviewed matched doi, peer-reviewed no doi, not peer-reviewed, retracted. The `doi_status` column holds the word `verify_doi.py` reports: matched, mismatch, resolves unchecked, unresolved, retracted, concern, preprint, no doi, error.
